Prayer for sleep and rest

Night can make unfinished work, fear, regret, and uncertainty feel louder. These guides focus on surrendering what cannot be solved tonight and creating a gentler transition into rest.

Pray about this nowOpen the Bible

Where to begin

A bedtime prayer does not need to be long. Review the day honestly, give thanks for one thing, name one burden you are carrying into the night, and ask for rest. Psalm 4, Psalm 23, Psalm 91, and Matthew 11 can be read slowly rather than used as formulas.

Rest also has practical dimensions. Regular sleep routines, reduced stimulation, and appropriate medical care can matter alongside prayer. If sleep problems are persistent or severe, seeking qualified help is compatible with faith.

Keep the next step simple

You do not need to solve the whole situation in one prayer. Name what is true, bring the specific burden to God, read the passage in context, and choose one faithful next step you can take today.

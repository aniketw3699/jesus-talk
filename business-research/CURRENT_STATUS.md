## Search direction

Look broadly across global industries and professional workflows, including obscure but real paid software markets.

Do not anchor on examples such as Adobe, Canva, Tally, Gmail, Cloudflare, GitHub or Vercel. Those examples only illustrate that the target can be any useful computer-related software category.

The desired business can be B2B or B2C, web, desktop, PWA or local application, provided:
- developer-side operating cost can remain near zero at small scale;
- there is existing willingness to pay;
- the problem is painful enough to create pull;
- there is a meaningful reason to choose us;
- a complete comparable free substitute does not already dominate;
- we can plausibly acquire users through search, free utilities, product-led growth or direct demand.

Offline Bible

An offline Bible reader that prepares itself in your browser

After an initial online preparation, 1into1 can keep the public-domain World English Bible available in browser storage so reading and local search can continue without a live Bible API.

Open the free sanctuary Open the Bible

All 66 books

The reader prepares the World English Bible book by book and stores the data in browser Cache Storage for later use.

No account required to read

Bible reading is not tied to subscription status. You can open chapters and search Scripture without creating an account.

No paid Bible API dependency

Displayed Scripture comes from a public-domain Bible dataset rather than generated AI text or a metered Bible API.

Works like a web app

You can use 1into1 in the browser, and supported browsers can install the PWA for a more app-like experience. Offline availability depends on the relevant files having been prepared on that device.

What works offline

  • Chapter reading from the prepared Bible library.
  • Direct verse lookup and local Scripture search.
  • Topic shortcuts backed by local Bible references.
  • Core local prayer experiences, journal, and selected guided journeys.
First visit matters: the device needs an internet connection while the Bible library and app shell are being prepared. If browser storage is cleared later, the device may need to prepare those files again.

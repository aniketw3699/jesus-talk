Surrender Control: Finding Peace When Leadership Feels Empty

Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest. (Matthew 11:28)

When the mantle of leadership feels hollow, the soul cries out for meaning, and the weight of expectations becomes a silent ache that no achievement can soothe.

Jesus invites us to lay down that burden, reminding us that true rest is found not in our own strength but in the gentle embrace of the One who carries our weariness.

In quiet stillness, breathe deeply, release control, and whisper a prayer of surrender, trusting that God’s peace will fill the empty spaces left behind.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I find rest when my leadership role feels empty?

Bring your weariness to Jesus as He invites, "Come to me," and allow His promise of rest to replace the emptiness with divine peace.

What does surrendering control look like in daily life?

It means pausing to pray, releasing your agenda to God, and trusting Him to guide each step, letting His Spirit fill the gaps where you once tried to go alone.

A Prayer for Severe Anxiety, Panic, and Racing Thoughts

“Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you. Not as the world gives do I give to you. Let not your hearts be troubled, neither let them be afraid.” (John 14:27)

When panic strikes and your chest tightens with the weight of unseen fears, the world demands that you strive to fix what feels broken. Yet divine peace does not begin with striving; it begins with letting go.

In this hour of restlessness, know that you do not carry this trial by your strength alone. Even when your breath grows shallow and your mind races ahead into tomorrow's uncertainty, an everlasting presence remains anchored within you.

Breathe quietly. Release the need to control what tomorrow will bring. Lay every racing thought at the foot of the Cross, and allow sacred stillness to take its place.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Bible verse helps immediately during a panic attack?

Philippians 4:6–7 reminds us: “Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.”

How can I find peace when my mind will not stop worrying?

Take slow, intentional breaths and focus solely on the present moment. Releasing the burden of tomorrow through spoken prayer allows the nervous system and the spirit to settle into stillness.

Security & confidentiality

Minimum data first.

The free pre-check requires no confidential project documents. If you choose to proceed to a full review, document handling is agreed before sensitive files are shared.

Before files are shared

Agree the handling expectations.

  • What documents are necessary for the agreed milestone scope
  • How the files will be transferred
  • Who is authorized to provide them
  • Any required retention or deletion expectations
Data minimization

Do not send more than the review needs.

A milestone review generally needs contractual scope, acceptance criteria, the payment request and evidence relevant to delivery. Personal data or unrelated commercial information should not be included unless it is genuinely necessary to assess the milestone.

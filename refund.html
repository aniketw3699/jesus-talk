REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

🕊️ 14-Day Refund Window: Eligible first-time Plus purchases may be requested for refund within 14 calendar days of the initial purchase, subject to applicable law and payment-provider rules.

1. Refund Eligibility

For our voluntary 14-day policy, the request must be made within 14 calendar days of the initial eligible Plus purchase. Renewal refunds and any additional statutory rights are handled according to applicable law and the payment provider’s terms.

2. Easy Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel an active Plus subscription using the subscription-management link provided by Lemon Squeezy. Unless otherwise stated at checkout or required by law, cancellation stops future renewal and access continues through the already-paid billing period.

3. How to Request a Refund

Email info@1into1.com with the email address used for purchase and, when available, the Lemon Squeezy order or receipt reference. We will review the request and respond as soon as reasonably possible.

YOUR PRIVACY COMES FIRST

Local-first by default, with cloud features only when you choose them.

Local prayer: Your local prayer conversations, journal, journeys, and Lay It Down experience stay on this device by default. Lay It Down text is never saved.

Ask Deeper: When you choose Ask Deeper, your question and limited recent context are sent to the configured cloud AI provider so it can answer.

Account: Signing in syncs only account/entitlement information by default. Plus encrypted backup is optional and must be started by you; backup content is encrypted in your browser before upload.

Analytics: We may collect product-usage events such as feature opens or mode changes. Prayer text is not deliberately included in those analytics events.

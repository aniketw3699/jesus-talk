Prayer without needing the perfect words

Start from what you are carrying—anxiety, grief, money pressure, relationships, forgiveness, loneliness, or simply not knowing what to pray. Local prayer experiences help you form a prayer and connect it to Scripture.

Unlimited local prayer is free

Ordinary prayer does not have a daily prayer quota. Core prayer, journal, journeys, Lay It Down, and the prepared Bible are designed to work on the device without requiring a paid cloud response.

Pay for cloud intelligence, not for prayer

Guests can try Ask Deeper and signed-in free users receive a daily allowance. Plus expands cloud study for normal personal use and can add optional encrypted cross-device backup; reasonable fair-use limits protect the paid cloud service from automated abuse.

Not an AI pretending to be Jesus

1into1 is a Scripture-guided companion. It does not claim to be Jesus, God, the Holy Spirit, clergy, or a source of divine revelation.

What you can do

  • Pray locally without a quota: choose Comfort, Written Prayer, or Life Guidance without requiring a cloud model for the core flow or an account.
  • Read the Bible: open the public-domain World English Bible, search it, and prepare it for offline reading.
  • Keep a local journal: your prayer journal can work without an account.
  • Follow guided journeys: structured tracks for anxiety, financial worry, and forgiveness.
  • Ask Deeper when you want it: use optional cloud reasoning for context, theology, and deeper Bible-study questions.

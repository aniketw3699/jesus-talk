Material findings

Three items prevent clean acceptance.

1. Data migration reconciliation — $2,500 unverified

The evidence supports completion of the migration activity, but the supplied pack does not demonstrate full reconciliation for one contracted dataset.

2. UAT completion — $3,000 unverified

Two material scenarios remain open. The milestone may be commercially acceptable with conditions, but the evidence does not support describing UAT as fully complete.

3. Reporting & automation — $6,000 unverified

No acceptance evidence was supplied for the reporting pack included in the milestone scope.

Questions for the implementation partner

What the buyer should ask next.

Please provide reconciliation evidence for the outstanding migrated dataset and confirm the agreed tolerance.
Please identify the owner, target date and acceptance path for the two open UAT scenarios.
Please provide the reporting/automation deliverables mapped to the contractual acceptance criteria, including evidence of business validation.
Review conclusion

Evidence status: insufficient for clean acceptance

The supplied evidence supports $38,500 of the $50,000 milestone value. $11,500 remains partially evidenced or unverified and should be clarified before clean acceptance.

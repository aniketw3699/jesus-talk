What we review

The documents your project already produces.

Commercial scope

SOW or scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, change requests and the milestone/payment request.

Delivery evidence

UAT results, test evidence, migration results, screenshots, sign-offs, training or handover evidence.

Open risk

Defect lists, unresolved issues, partial acceptance items, missing evidence and disputed deliverables.

Output

One evidence chain, not another project status report.

The review answers a practical question: what portion of the milestone is clearly supported by the evidence provided, and what still requires clarification before clean acceptance?

Promise → evidence

  • Deliverable-by-deliverable evidence status
  • Acceptance-criteria traceability
  • UAT and defect context
  • Missing or ambiguous evidence called out explicitly

Evidence → money

  • Requested milestone value
  • Value associated with evidenced deliverables
  • Value associated with partially evidenced or unverified items
  • Questions to send back to the implementation partner

Decision boundary

Evidence assessment, not legal advice.

Our report describes the documentary evidence available to support implementation acceptance. It does not determine legal rights, statutory compliance or whether a payment must or must not be made. The buyer retains the final decision.

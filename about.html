About 1into1

A narrow service for a high-stakes implementation decision.

1into1 is an independent buyer-side implementation assurance service focused on one question: has the implementation vendor provided enough evidence for the buyer to consider a milestone cleanly accepted?

Our focus

Evidence before acceptance.

We sit above the project's existing tools and teams. We do not replace the ERP/CRM platform, implementation partner, project manager, TestRail/Jira, finance team, lawyer or auditor. We create an independent evidence map for the buyer.

Current stage

Founder-led pilot reviews.

Current reviews are led directly by founder Aniket Gawande. The service is intentionally manual at this stage so the methodology can be tested and improved on real implementation evidence before more of the workflow is automated.

Why 1into1 exists

Implementation delivery is often reported in project language. Payment is a buyer decision.

A buyer may receive status reports, UAT results, defect lists and vendor updates, yet still have to answer a commercial question: what has actually been proven delivered against the acceptance conditions connected to this milestone?

Independent

We are not the implementation vendor whose milestone is being assessed.

Buyer-side

The review is designed around the organization accepting the work and releasing payment.

Explainable

Findings are tied back to the evidence supplied rather than hidden behind a proprietary score.

What we are not

No inflated claims.

  • Not a law firm
  • Not a statutory audit practice
  • Not an ERP reseller or implementation partner
  • Not a substitute for the buyer's contractual, financial or legal advisers

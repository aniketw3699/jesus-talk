PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

🕊️ Sacred Privacy Commitment: We believe prayer communion and spiritual confession are strictly confidential. We do not sell your data, build advertising profiles, or store your personal prayer dialogues on permanent database records.

1. Information We Process & Store

When you use You With Jesus, we handle only the minimal information strictly required to provide pastoral continuity, fair quota management, and account access:

  • Local Device Storage: Your full conversational feed, journal archives, and intake selections remain stored directly within your device's browser (localStorage). You retain complete authority to wipe this history anytime with one click.
  • Ephemeral Sanctuary & Confessions: Prayers submitted in the chat and burdens released in the Gethsemane Surrender tool are processed ephemerally over encrypted TLS connections. They dissolve immediately upon output and are never logged, inspected, or used to train artificial intelligence models.
  • Anonymous Quotas & Abuse Prevention: To enforce daily prayer limits for guests and shield our sanctuary against automated abuse, transient client IP addresses are temporarily recorded in a rate-limiting registry (guest_usage) and purged routinely.
  • Account & Spiritual Walk Metrics: If you sign in via Google Authentication, we securely sync high-level non-confessional journey attributes—such as your prayer habit streak, persistent prayer intentions, an AI-derived spiritual state summary (e.g., "seeking peace"), and active subscription status—strictly to maintain continuity across your devices.

2. Payment & Billing Information

All subscription transactions and one-time devotional passes are processed directly by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy. We never view, collect, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking credentials on our infrastructure.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure Providers

We work with trusted cloud and platform providers adhering to rigorous data security and encryption standards:

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): Authentication, hosting, and secure security-rule-guarded entitlement storage.
  • Groq Inc.: Secure, low-latency inference processing without long-term prompt retention.
  • Lemon Squeezy: PCI-DSS compliant payment processing and subscription lifecycle webhooks.

4. Data Rights & Contact

In accordance with GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy frameworks, you hold the right to review, export, or request complete erasure of your account record and associated profile metrics. For any data inquiries or deletion requests, contact our pastoral technical team at info@1into1.com.

PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

🕊️ Zero-Knowledge Sanctuary: We believe communion and personal prayers are sacred. We do not sell your data, build ad profiles, or store your private chat dialogues on permanent database records.

1. Information We Process

When you use You With Jesus, we handle minimal information strictly necessary to provide the service:

  • Local Device Storage: Your prayer histories, reflection journals, and intake answers live in your local browser storage (localStorage). You can clear this anytime with one tap.
  • Authentication Data: If you sign in via Google Auth, we receive your email and basic display name to manage access credits.
  • Ephemeral Chat Processing: Messages submitted to the chat are transmitted via secure HTTPS encryption to our inference engine and discarded after generation.

2. Payment & Billing Information

All subscription payments and billing transactions are processed directly by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy. We never store, receive, or process your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking credentials.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted infrastructure providers that adhere to high security standards:

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): Authentication and hosting infrastructure.
  • Groq Inc.: Ultra-low latency language model inference.
  • Lemon Squeezy: PCI-DSS compliant payment processing.

4. Contact & Data Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA standards, you have the right to request deletion of your account record. To exercise this or ask privacy questions, contact our developer support team at anuanuu87@gmail.com.
